Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO (berechtigtes Interesse an der Beantwortung von Anfragen). Zur statistischen Auswertung der Besucherzahlen nutze ich Plausible Analytics (Plausible Insights OÜ, Västriku tn 2, 50403 Tartu, Estland). Erfasst werden u. a. aufgerufene Seiten, Referrer, Land, Gerätetyp sowie anonyme Ereignisse (z. B. „Spielmodus gestartet“). Die Daten werden auf Servern in der EU verarbeitet. Rechtsgrundlage ist Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO (berechtigtes Interesse an einer datensparsamen Reichweitenanalyse).

Ihre Auswahl von Sprache (EN/DE) und Farbschema (hell/dunkel) wird im localStorage Ihres Browsers gespeichert, damit sie beim nächsten Besuch erhalten bleibt. Diese Daten verlassen Ihr Gerät nicht. Die Speicherung ist für den von Ihnen gewünschten Dienst erforderlich (§ 25 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 TDDDG). Sie können sie jederzeit über die Browsereinstellungen löschen.

6. Schriftarten

Alle Schriftarten werden lokal von diesem Server ausgeliefert. Es findet keine Verbindung zu Google Fonts oder anderen Schriftanbietern statt.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure and stable operation). Vercel is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; in addition, a data processing agreement (Data Processing Addendum) including Standard Contractual Clauses is in place.

3. Contact form and email

If you use the contact form, your name, email address and message are processed by a server function at Vercel and delivered to me as an email via the email delivery service Resend (Resend, Inc., 2261 Market Street #5039, San Francisco, CA 94114, USA). The data is not stored on the website itself. Resend is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; a data processing agreement is in place. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures, e.g. job or project enquiries) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in responding to enquiries). The data is deleted once the enquiry has been fully dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

To analyse visitor numbers statistically, I use Plausible Analytics (Plausible Insights OÜ, Västriku tn 2, 50403 Tartu, Estonia). The IP address is only used briefly to generate an anonymous hash that changes daily, and is not stored. Data collected includes pages visited, referrer, country, device type and anonymous events (e.g. “game mode started”). The data is processed on servers in the EU. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in privacy-friendly audience measurement).

Your choice of language (EN/DE) and colour scheme (light/dark) is saved in your browser’s localStorage so that it is kept for your next visit. This data does not leave your device. The storage is strictly necessary to provide the service you requested (Section 25(2) no. 2 of the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act, TDDDG). You can delete it at any time via your browser settings.

6. Fonts

All fonts are served locally from this server. No connection is made to Google Fonts or any other font provider.

7. External links

Links to GitHub and LinkedIn are only loaded when you click them. From then on, the privacy policy of the respective provider applies.
